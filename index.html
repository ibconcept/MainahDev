What to expect from a first client meetup

A relaxed, focused conversation to understand the problem before proposing a solution. No pressure and no jargon—just a useful next step.

  1. Your goals: what you are building, who it serves, and how success will look.
  2. Scope & priorities: essential features, design needs, timeline, and constraints.
  3. A clear follow-up: initial recommendations, what I need from you, and a written proposal or quote when useful.
