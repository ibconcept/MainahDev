Rates & Pricing

Every project has a different scope, so I prepare a clear, tailored quote after we understand the goals. There are no one-size-fits-all prices listed here; the written proposal confirms deliverables, timeline, and cost before work begins.

What can be scoped

  • Websites, web applications & software development
  • Graphic design, visual identity & brand assets
  • UX research, UI design, prototypes & design systems
  • Improvements, integrations & ongoing support

How a quote works

  1. 1. Discovery: share your goals, audience, required features, and timeline.
  2. 2. Scope: we agree on deliverables and priorities, including what is out of scope.
  3. 3. Proposal: receive a written estimate with milestones, payment schedule, and timeline.
  4. 4. Approval: work starts once the scope and terms are confirmed.

Need a quick estimate? Send a short brief and mention your preferred budget range. It helps shape a realistic scope; a first conversation does not commit you to a project.
